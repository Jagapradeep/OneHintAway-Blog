Bubble sort explained: swap neighbours that are out of order, and stop after a sweep with no swaps (LeetCode 3011 practice).

Each sweep carries the biggest unsorted value to the end, like a bubble rising.
The trap: put the early-stop check inside the inner loop and 5 2 4 1 3 stops at 2 4 1 3 5.
Practice: LeetCode 2418 (easy) and LeetCode 3011 (medium).
